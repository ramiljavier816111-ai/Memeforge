FAQ

Quick answers

Should I revoke the authorities?

It's optional. Revoking mint authority fixes your supply forever, and revoking freeze authority means holders can't be frozen. Buyers often look for both. Revoking is permanent, so decide before you create.

Why can't anyone trade it yet?

Creating a token only mints it. Trading needs a liquidity pool, which you create on a DEX like Raydium.

Is this mainnet?

The creator is currently set up for Devnet, which uses free test SOL. Devnet tokens have no real value.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Tap Connect, choose your wallet, and the page opens inside the wallet's browser. The site must be hosted on https for that to work.

Is a memecoin safe to buy or launch?

Memecoins are extremely volatile and many go to zero. Nothing here is financial advice, so only use money you can afford to lose and follow the laws where you live.
